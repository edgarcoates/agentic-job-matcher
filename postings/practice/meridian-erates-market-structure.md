## About the role
Meridian operates a multi-dealer electronic trading venue for government bonds and swaps. Graduate
Analysts join the Rates product team and rotate across (1) market structure & liquidity analytics,
(2) client workflow and API onboarding with buy-side and dealer clients, and (3) product pricing
and fee strategy. Analysts present recommendations to the product committee from year one, and
the strongest take ownership of a product line (e.g. a new protocol or data product) within three years.
We are building AI-assisted analytics for liquidity and execution quality; analysts are expected to
use and extend these tools. There is a well-trodden path to our New York office.

## Requirements
- Graduating in 2027 with a strong quantitative degree (economics, finance, maths or similar)
- Genuine interest in market structure, fixed income and electronic trading
- Strong Excel; Python or SQL a plus (training provided)
- Clear communicator, comfortable with clients
- Right to work in the UK
